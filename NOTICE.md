# Notice and provenance

This repository is a curated handoff bundle. It intentionally does not include competition data, hidden test data, model weights, LoRA adapters, or credentials.

## Third-party material

Some scripts and method descriptions are adaptations or packaging of publicly available Kaggle notebooks and datasets. Their upstream authors, licenses, attribution requirements, and competition rules remain applicable. In particular, see the links in `docs/PUBLIC_SOURCES.md` before redistribution or commercial use.

Known upstream references include:

- Name That LLM competition data and rules: Kaggle
- Qwen model inputs and Qwen-derived checkpoints: Qwen/Kaggle model sources
- Two-stage LoRA public pool recipe: Nishant Dahal's public Kaggle notebook and dataset
- Style-aware n-gram recipe: Yang Kuang Ou's public Kaggle notebook
- Additional public pools and checkpoints listed in `docs/PUBLIC_SOURCES.md`

Do not upload the upstream datasets or checkpoints into this repository. Attach them through Kaggle inputs or retrieve them from their official public source under the applicable license.
